# Art of Dota: Privacy PolicyLast updated: November 4, 2026Art of Dota is a personal tool run by the owner of the Art of Dota / PurexOwnage YouTube channel. It is used only to upload and schedule videos to the owner's own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts.## What data the app accessesWhen the owner signs in with Google, the app receives permission to upload videos and manage videos, thumbnails and playlists on the owner's own YouTube channel (YouTube Data API scopes youtube.upload and youtube).## How it is used and storedAccess tokens are stored only on the owner's personal computer and are used solely to publish the owner's own videos. No data is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or collected from anyone other than the owner.Art of Dota's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.## Revoking accessAccess can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.## Contacttheharrisontruong@gmail.com
